You are choosing the next swipe (up, down, left, right) in a Threes-like 4x4 sliding-tile game. Decision rule, applied in order: (a) do not move your largest tile off its cell if any other swipe is available (use the 'max tile displaced' fact); (b) among the remaining options, remove the most 1s and 2s from the board (use the 'worst tiles removed' and 'worst tile has partner' facts); (c) then pick the option with the most merges. If none of these distinguish the options, pick the one leaving the most empty cells. All numbers in the options are precomputed facts about what each swipe does; pick the single best option.
